# Renova LDN — Site Features

## The Website

The public-facing site has three pages that visitors can browse.

**Home**
The main landing page. It introduces the business, shows a summary of services, highlights why customers should choose Renova LDN, walks through the process (Enquire → Quote → Schedule → Complete), and includes a call-to-action to get a free quote.

**Services**
Lists all 15 services with a filter bar so visitors can browse by category (Interior, Exterior, Structural). Each service has an icon, a short description, and a link to get in touch.

**Contact**
A form where visitors fill in their name, email, phone, the service they need, a message, and their preferred way to be contacted (email or phone). When submitted, the enquiry is saved and the visitor sees a confirmation message.

## The Admin Panel

Access the admin at `/admin`. Log in with your admin credentials.

### Leads

Every time someone fills in the contact form, it shows up here as a lead.

- **View a lead** — click on any row to see the full details: contact info, what service they need, their message, and any notes you've added.
- **Update the status** — mark a lead as New, Contacted, or Completed so you always know where things stand.
- **Add internal notes** — write private notes on a lead (the customer never sees these).
- **Email the customer** — hit the Email button on any lead to open a pre-filled email in your mail app.
- **Delete a lead** — remove it if it's spam or no longer relevant.
- **Filter & search** — filter leads by status or service, and search by name, email, or phone.

The dashboard badge on the Leads menu shows how many new (unread) enquiries you have.

### Dashboard

The first thing you see when you log in. Shows five numbers at a glance:

- Total leads ever received
- How many are new (need a response)
- How many are in progress (contacted)
- How many are completed
- How many came in this month

### Site Settings

Found in the sidebar under **Site Settings**. Change any of the following and it updates across the whole website instantly — no code needed:

- Phone number
- Email address
- Address / service area
- Weekday opening hours
- Weekend opening hours
- Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn profile links
